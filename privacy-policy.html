Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

Milestone Premier Insurance Services (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information when you use this website, submit a quote request, communicate with us, or participate in our text messaging program.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number, mailing address, and contact preferences;
  • Details about your household, property, vehicles, business, coverage interests, existing insurance, and insurance needs;
  • Information contained in quote forms, emails, calls, text messages, documents, or other communications; and
  • Records of consent, communication preferences, and opt-in or opt-out activity.

We and providers supporting the website may automatically receive technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages visited, and approximate dates and times of access.

How We Collect Information

We collect information when you submit a form, call, email, text, or otherwise interact with us. We may also receive information from service providers that support form processing, website operations, communications, analytics, customer service, and business systems. The quote form embedded on this website is hosted by Jotform and is also subject to Jotform’s applicable privacy practices.

How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and assist with insurance quote requests;
  • Communicate about products, services, appointments, documents, policy service, and customer care;
  • Evaluate or route requests to appropriate insurance carriers, licensed professionals, or service providers;
  • Operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website and our business processes;
  • Maintain business and consent records, honor communication choices, and meet legal or regulatory responsibilities; and
  • Identify or prevent fraud, misuse, security incidents, or unauthorized activity.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

We and our service providers may use cookies, pixels, tags, and comparable technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, understand usage, and measure performance. Your browser may allow you to restrict cookies. Doing so can affect the operation of the embedded quote form or other site features.

How We Disclose Information

We do not sell or rent personal information for money. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to insurance carriers, licensed insurance professionals, form and hosting providers, communications vendors, analytics or customer management providers, professional advisers, and other service providers that assist with a request or perform services for our business.

We may also disclose information when required by law, regulation, subpoena, legal process, or governmental request; to protect the rights, property, or safety of Milestone Premier Insurance Services, clients, or others; or as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, transfer, or sale of some or all of the business, subject to applicable law.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and text messaging consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We may provide this information to service providers that help deliver messages, maintain consent records, or operate communication systems, solely to perform those services on our behalf. The other disclosure categories in this Policy do not include text messaging originator opt-in data and consent for third-party marketing.

Calls and Text Messages

If you provide a telephone number and affirmatively opt in, you consent to calls and text messages from Milestone Premier Insurance Services about quote requests, account notifications, customer care, policy service, appointment coordination, and marketing where permitted by law. Messages may be sent using automated technology. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of text messages or HELP for assistance. Review the complete SMS Terms & Conditions.

Email Choices

We may use your email address to answer questions, provide quote or service information, and send marketing communications where permitted. You can opt out of promotional email through any unsubscribe method included in a message or by contacting us. We may continue to send non-promotional messages related to an active request, policy, or service.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in this Policy, including serving clients, maintaining business and consent records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and satisfying legal, regulatory, tax, or insurance-related requirements. Retention periods vary with the nature of the information and our obligations.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No internet transmission or electronic storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Links and Services

This website may link to or embed services operated by other organizations. Their privacy, security, and content practices are governed by their own policies, and we are not responsible for those practices. Review applicable third-party terms before providing information.

Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. If you believe a child submitted information, contact us so we can review and address the request.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to ask about, access, correct, or delete certain personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity, and legal exceptions may permit or require us to retain certain information.

Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. A revised version will appear on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of the website after an update is subject to the revised Policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy choices, or communication preferences may be directed to Milestone Premier Insurance Services at 209-259-0052 or amcdonald3@farmersagent.com. We serve clients across California.
